The outcome of adding or inserting items. Items that could not be resolved are skipped and named in "unresolved".

addedinteger (minimum: 1)YesHow many items were added. Never zero; a call that adds nothing returns an error.
unresolvedarray of Playlist.UnresolvedItemYesOne entry per item that was not added, in request order. Empty when every item was added.
