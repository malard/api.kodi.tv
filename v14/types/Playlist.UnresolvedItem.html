Playlist.UnresolvedItem

An item that was not added, and why.

NameTypeRequiredDefaultDescription
itemPlaylist.ItemYesThe item exactly as the client asked for it.
reason"notfound" | "unavailable" | "invalid"Yes"notfound": the item does not exist. "unavailable": it exists but cannot be reached at the moment, so the reference is worth keeping and retrying. "invalid": the item cannot go in a playlist of this type, or holds nothing playable.
